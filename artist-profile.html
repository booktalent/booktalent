About Priya
Priya Sharma is one of Mumbai's most celebrated Bollywood vocalists with over 8 years of professional performance experience. Known for her silky smooth voice and stage presence, she has headlined over 300 events across India, UAE and the UK.

Trained under the Jaipur Gharana classical tradition, Priya seamlessly blends classical roots with modern Bollywood, Sufi and ghazal genres. She has performed at ITC Maratha, Taj Lands End, and NSCI Mumbai for clients ranging from intimate private celebrations to corporate galas of 2,000+ guests.

Priya brings her own professional PA system and backup vocalist for all performances. She also offers customised setlists, song dedications and special medley arrangements on request.

Artist FAQs
Do you bring your own sound system?
Yes! For the Premium and Royal packages I bring a full professional PA system (2000W, QSC powered speakers). For the Acoustic Solo package I carry a portable Bose system suitable for rooms up to 150 people.
Can I request specific songs?
Absolutely. All packages include custom song dedications. For the Royal Concert package, we build the entire setlist together. Please share your song wishlist at least 1 week before the event.
Do you travel outside Mumbai?
Yes, I perform pan-India and have also done international shows in Dubai, Singapore and the UK. Travel and accommodation charges apply for out-of-city bookings.
What is the cancellation policy?
Cancellations made 15+ days before the event receive a full refund. 7–14 days: 50% refund. Less than 7 days: the 5% token is non-refundable. In case I cancel, you receive a 100% refund + priority rebooking.
