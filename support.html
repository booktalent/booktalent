How do I book an artist on BookTalent?
+
Booking an artist is a simple 5-step process: (1) Browse and select your artist, (2) Pick a date and time slot, (3) Fill in your event details, (4) Review everything, and (5) Pay the 5% booking token to confirm.

Once you pay, the artist is notified instantly, an auto-generated contract is created, and funds are held securely in escrow until after the event. Try booking now →

Can I cancel or reschedule my booking?
+
Yes, you can cancel or reschedule based on our policy:

• 15+ days before event: Full refund of the advance token
• 7–14 days before event: 50% refund of the token
• Less than 7 days: The 5% token is non-refundable
• Rescheduling: Free reschedule if artist is available on the new date (up to 7 days before event)

To cancel, go to My Bookings → Select booking → Cancel Booking. Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days.

What if the artist cancels my booking?
+
In the unlikely event that an artist cancels, BookTalent provides full protection: You'll receive a 100% refund of all payments made, priority access to re-book a comparable or better artist, and a ₹500 platform credit as goodwill. Artist cancellations are rare — our 98% completion rate speaks for itself.

What is the 5% booking token and how does payment work?
+
The 5% booking token is a small advance fee (5% of the total package cost including GST) that you pay to secure the artist's availability on your chosen date. Here's how the full payment flow works:

1. Token (5%): Paid at booking confirmation — goes to platform escrow
2. Remaining 95%: Due before the event (milestone or one-time, per agreement)
3. After the event: You verify completion, and the artist receives their payout automatically

All payments are GST-inclusive and a full invoice is auto-generated.

How long does a refund take?
+
Refund timelines depend on the payment method used:

• UPI: Instant to 2 hours
• Credit / Debit Card: 5–7 business days
• Net Banking: 3–5 business days
• BookTalent Wallet: Instant

Once your refund is approved by our team, you'll receive an email confirmation with the exact amount and timeline.

How do I join BookTalent as an artist?
+
Joining is free and takes about 10 minutes: (1) Create an Artist account, (2) Complete your profile with photos, videos and bio, (3) Set your pricing packages, (4) Submit your KYC documents for verification, (5) Go live and start receiving booking requests.

Once verified, your profile appears in search results across India. Our team reviews applications within 24–48 hours.

When and how do artists receive payouts?
+
Artist payouts work as follows:

1. Client pays the full amount before the event (held in escrow)
2. After the event is marked complete by the client, funds are released
3. Platform commission (typically 12–18% based on tier) is deducted
4. Net amount is credited to your BookTalent wallet within 24 hours
5. Withdraw to your bank account anytime — NEFT (1–2 days) or UPI (instant)

There are no withdrawal fees. GST invoice is auto-generated for every booking.

What documents do I need for KYC verification?
+
For KYC verification you need to provide:

Identity Proof (any one): Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, or Voter ID
Address Proof (any one): Aadhaar, Utility bill (last 3 months), Bank statement
Bank Account Details: Cancelled cheque or bank statement
Artist-specific (recommended): Performance portfolio, YouTube/Instagram links

KYC is reviewed within 24–48 hours. You'll be notified by email and SMS upon approval.

How do I reset my password?
+
To reset your password: go to the Sign In page, click "Forgot Password?", enter your registered email or phone number, and we'll send a secure reset link. The link expires in 15 minutes. You can also use OTP login as an alternative if you can't access your email.
