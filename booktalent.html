Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does BookTalent verify artists? +
Every artist goes through a multi-step KYC process including government ID verification, portfolio review, video audition and reference checks. Verified artists display a gold badge on their profile.
What is the 5% booking token? +
To secure an artist's availability, you pay just 5% of the total fee as a platform booking token. The remaining 95% is collected via milestones or before the event, and held in escrow until completion.
What if the artist cancels or doesn't show up? +
BookTalent provides full refund protection. If an artist cancels within 48 hours of the event or fails to perform, you receive a 100% refund plus priority rebooking assistance with a comparable artist.
Can I negotiate pricing with artists? +
Yes! You can send a custom booking request with your budget. Artists can counter-offer or accept. Our platform facilitates transparent negotiation while protecting both parties with an auto-generated contract.
Are there discounts for corporate or bulk bookings? +
Absolutely. Corporate clients and agencies get access to volume pricing, dedicated account managers, priority support and custom contract terms. Contact our corporate team for a customized proposal.
